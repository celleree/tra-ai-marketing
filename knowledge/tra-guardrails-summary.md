# TRA Guardrails — Implementation Summary

This file is a concise coding-context summary. The approved TRA guardrails supplied to the project remain authoritative for exact wording and claim review.

Core rules for creative generation:

- Do not fabricate guarantees, customer outcomes, dollar amounts, statistics, endorsements, government affiliation, or competitor claims.
- Financial, legal, tax-resolution, savings, settlement, outcome, comparative, statistical, and performance claims require approved supporting material or human review.
- Do not create fake testimonials or combine multiple customer stories into one person.
- Do not use customer names, quotes, stories, outcomes, or statistics in advertising unless TRA has confirmed the required evidence and permission.
- Do not imply TRA is affiliated with the IRS or another government agency unless explicitly approved and sourced.
- AI output is not automatically approved.
- Unsupported claims must remain unknown rather than being inferred.
- Deterministic enforcement should be used where practical.

When exact disclaimer text or exact compliance wording is required, use the approved TRA guardrails source rather than inventing or paraphrasing it.
