# TRA Customer Insights

Derived from TRA five-star customer reviews. Use this summary for customer language, creative strategy, personas, objections, and proof themes. The raw review source remains external and should be consulted only when exact evidence is required.

Do not treat review themes as permission to invent outcomes, testimonials, financial claims, or guarantees.

## Recurring problems

- Stress, fear, and uncertainty around IRS or tax problems.
- Feeling overwhelmed trying to handle a tax issue alone.
- Unexpected IRS notices or back-tax discoveries.
- Difficulty understanding the process or knowing what to do next.
- Skepticism about tax-relief companies, pricing, pressure, and legitimacy.
- Frustration with poor communication or lack of updates.

## Desired outcomes

- Peace of mind and reduced uncertainty.
- Clear next steps and straightforward explanations.
- Confidence that a competent team is handling the situation.
- Responsive, professional, no-pressure communication.
- Feeling informed, supported, and cared for rather than treated like a transaction.

## Proof and trust themes

Customers repeatedly value knowledgeable representatives, clear explanations, responsiveness, professionalism, patience, reassurance, friendliness, thoroughness, straightforward communication, and timely follow-up.

Useful voice-of-customer concepts include: `peace of mind`, `explained everything clearly`, `answered all of my questions`, `knowledgeable`, `professional`, `responsive`, `made me feel comfortable`, `no pressure`, `straightforward help`, and `timely`.

Use these as inspiration, not fabricated quotations or attributed testimonials.

## Useful personas

- **Overwhelmed taxpayer:** needs a calm, simple path forward.
- **Skeptical buyer:** worries about legitimacy, pressure, hidden fees, or poor service.
- **DIY taxpayer who hit a wall:** tried handling the issue alone and now wants help.
- **Unexpected-notice customer:** received an IRS notice and needs clarity quickly.
- **High-stress customer:** is handling a difficult life situation alongside a tax problem.
- **Clarity-seeker:** wants someone to explain the process and communicate consistently.

These are creative personas inferred from recurring situations, not demographic claims about TRA's customer base.

## Strong creative directions

- Problem: stress, confusion, IRS notices, unresolved tax issues.
- Desired outcome: clarity, confidence, support, peace of mind.
- Objection: whether tax-relief companies are legitimate or high pressure.
- Objection: whether communication and updates will be reliable.
- Proof: knowledgeable, responsive advocates and clear explanations.
- Emotional: moving from overwhelmed to supported.
- Educational: what happens after someone asks TRA for help.
- Persona: someone who tried handling a tax issue alone or just received an IRS notice.

## Guardrails

- Never create a fake testimonial or named customer story.
- Never combine multiple customer stories and present them as one real person's experience.
- Do not imply universal outcomes.
- Do not turn an individual result into a general guarantee.
- Do not use customer names or exact quotes in advertising without confirmed permission for that use.
- Separate service-quality proof from financial or tax-resolution claims.
- Prefer clarity, responsiveness, professionalism, confidence, and peace-of-mind themes when outcome claims are not separately approved.
