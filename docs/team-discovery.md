# Marketing Team Discovery

Ask each stakeholder a short set of questions:

1. What do you spend too much time doing in Meta Ads Manager?
2. What questions do you repeatedly ask about campaign performance?
3. What reports or comparisons are annoying to build manually?
4. What creative ideas do you save but rarely have time to produce?
5. What makes a creative obviously unusable for TRA?
6. Which claims, offers, or wording always require extra review?
7. If an AI media-buying assistant worked perfectly, what would you ask it every day?
