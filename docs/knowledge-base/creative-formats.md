# TRA Creative Formats

Creative categories are the primary diversity mechanism. Formats are simpler presentation structures used to express a category.

## Approved Formats

- `direct-response` - problem, offer, outcome, or strong headline led.
- `proof` - testimonial, review, statistic, authority, or evidence led.
- `educational` - explanation, FAQ, objection handling, reasons why, editorial, or news-style.
- `comparison-transformation` - comparison, us-vs-them, or before/after structure.
- `native-social` - meme, social-post, story-like, or platform-native presentation.

## Rules

- Each generated creative gets one primary category and one format.
- Categories should drive conceptual diversity.
- Formats should not be treated as separate messaging ideas.
- Visual execution is flexible and may include treatments such as tweet-style, review screenshot, plain-text hook, editorial, visual metaphor, checklist, nutrition-label style, or other original layouts.
- Do not use a secondary-format field.

See `docs/creative-categories.md` for the canonical 15 creative categories.
