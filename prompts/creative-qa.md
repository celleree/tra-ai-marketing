# Prompt: Creative QA

Review a generated TRA static ad before human selection.

Check:
- Factual accuracy against the TRA knowledge base
- Approved claims and offer language
- Required disclaimer presence
- Spelling and grammar
- Brand fit
- Clear CTA
- Obvious visual errors
- Duplicate or near-duplicate concept risk

Return either:
- PASS - ready for human review
- FAIL - list the exact problems to fix
