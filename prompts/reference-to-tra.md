# Prompt: Reference Ad to TRA Concept

## Inputs

- Reference creative
- Short note about why the reference is interesting
- TRA offer or campaign goal
- Number of variations requested

## Instructions

1. Analyze the reference for its underlying structure, not its brand-specific wording.
2. Identify the hook, layout, visual hierarchy, emotional angle, CTA style, and why the concept may attract attention.
3. Create original TRA-specific concepts inspired by the structure.
4. Use only approved TRA claims, offers, and compliance language from the knowledge base.
5. Do not copy protected brand elements, logos, or distinctive wording from the reference.
6. Return concise creative briefs ready for an image-generation model.
